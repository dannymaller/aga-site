Welcome, Neighbor!

The Avondale Gardening Alliance is a network of neighbors in and around the Avondale neighborhood of Chicago. We're so glad you stopped by! Use the menu to find information about our events, projects, and resources.

We need your help with the Fair!

The AGA Garden and Agriculture Fair (AGAF) is on Sunday, 8/30, and we are looking for volunteers and demonstrators! Let us know you're interested by filling out this form!

There are so many different ways to get involved:

  • Host a fair stop if you live in within the boundaries of N Kedzie (W) and N Rockwell (E), and between Roscoe (N) and the Kennedy/Diversey (S).
  • Demonstrate a skill, help coordinate the raffle, share your favorite garden recipe and new ways to use our plants, promote the event, curate a host site, or support the AGAF in another way!
  • Lend a hand with event support or even at the after-party!
